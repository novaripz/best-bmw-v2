Top 3 Best BMWs

These are my top three favorite BMWs. I chose them because of their performance, design, and how fun they are to drive.

  1. BMW M5 CS

    The BMW M5 CS is my favorite BMW. It is very fast while still being a four-door sedan. I also like its aggressive design.

    Image source: Wikimedia Commons

  2. BMW M4 Competition

    The BMW M4 Competition is one of my favorite BMW coupes. It has a lot of power and good handling. I also like the way the newer M4 looks.

    Image source: Wikimedia Commons

  3. BMW M3 Competition

    The BMW M3 Competition is a good mix of performance and practicality. It is fast but can still be used as an everyday car.

    Image source: Wikimedia Commons
